# Source Policy

Financial facts must come from primary authorities. Secondary blogs are not enough for material YMYL claims.

## Hierarchy

1. Medicare: Medicare.gov, CMS.gov
2. Social Security: SSA.gov
3. Federal tax: IRS.gov
4. Securities / investments: SEC.gov, Investor.gov, FINRA.org
5. Insurance: NAIC, state Departments of Insurance, insurer documentation when a specific product is discussed
6. Government economic data: Federal Reserve, BLS, Treasury
7. Secondary sources only to supplement, never to invent a number

## Required ledger fields

For every material claim:

- CLAIM
- SOURCE (official URL)
- SOURCE DATE
- ACCESSED
- ARTICLE SECTION
- STATUS: VERIFIED | OMIT | NEEDS HUMAN REVIEW

## Rules

- Never invent studies, statistics, URLs, IRS rules, CMS numbers, premiums, contribution limits, market returns, tax rates, or quotes.
- Time-sensitive figures (Medicare premiums, ACA subsidy rules, contribution limits) require a review date and a next-review date.
- Tax and legal concepts must state that individual circumstances vary.
- Estate-tax or restructuring analysis (including *Connelly v. United States*) requires a named CPA or attorney sign-off, or it is omitted.
- Draft models may not introduce quantitative facts outside the approved source pack.
