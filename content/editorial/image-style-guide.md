# Image Style Guide

LeadStar images should feel like premium financial-services editorial photography: natural expressions, realistic environments, soft professional lighting, navy/neutral alignment, human relationships, clarity, confidence, and understated luxury.

## Classification

Every image is one of: REAL PHOTOGRAPH | STAGED / COMPOSITE | AI-GENERATED | ILLUSTRATIVE / STOCK.

Do not caption AI, composite, or stock images as real clients, events, licenses, or offices.

## Jobs

Every image has one job: EMOTIONAL | EXPLANATORY | HUMANIZING. If it has no job, omit it.

## Count

Default: one hero image plus 0–2 useful inline visuals.

## Process

1. Write an image brief (subject, environment, tone, composition, lighting, palette, negatives, aspect, alt, caption).
2. Review the brief.
3. Generate or select the image.
4. Store a master at least 1200px wide plus 16:9, 4:3, and 1:1 derivatives when practical.
5. Use a descriptive filename such as `retirement-income-planning-conversation.webp`.

## Hard prohibitions

- Never depict Esther with AI. Use a verified real portrait only.
- Never present AI or stock imagery as real clients, staff, offices, or events.
- Never pair AI imagery with testimonials or outcome claims.
- No government emblems, Medicare cards, or official-looking seals near Medicare content.
- No fake readable policy text, statements, or charts.
- No piggy banks, gold coins, floating dollar signs, robot imagery, handshake-only concepts, or staged thumbs-up photos.

## Captions

Human, concise, tasteful, relevant. Neutral for illustrative images.

## Performance

Serve through Next Image. Prefer WebP/AVIF. Do not ship multi-megabyte PNGs on article pages.
