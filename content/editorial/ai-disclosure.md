# AI content disclosure

Internal workflow for every article:

1. Research and source ledger
2. SEO/editorial brief
3. Human review of the brief
4. AI-assisted or editor-assisted draft constrained to the source pack
5. Editorial review
6. Compliance review
7. Signed professional review attestation by Esther Francis before the public byline says “Reviewed by”
8. Human publish

Reader-facing copy currently says review attestation is pending unless `reviewAttestation` is `signed`.

An optional sentence, if approved later:

> Articles are drafted with research assistance and reviewed by a licensed insurance professional before publication.

Do not add a scary “written by artificial intelligence” banner. Do not add a consumer-facing chatbot without a separate compliance review.
